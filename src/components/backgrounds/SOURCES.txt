SceneSense background catalog sources

AeroShards, GradientWaves, MagicRings and Topography were integrated from the full React Bits component source supplied by the user.
GradientASCII was fetched from https://reactbits.dev/r/GradientASCII-JS-CSS.json.
Original Topographic Flow source: https://reactdrops.dev/r/topographic-flow.json (license retained separately).

Integration adaptations:
- AeroShards pins vgpu 0.4.1 for the supplied raw-WGSL/surface API.
- AeroShards and Topography allow click effects with mouseInteraction disabled.
- Topography focus remains stationary when mouseInteraction is disabled.
- Configurations disable hover effects, parallax and cursor-following.
- GPU pixel ratios are capped at 1.25; only one selected background mounts.
- The catalog thumbnails are static, not additional running canvases.
